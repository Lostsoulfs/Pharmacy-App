# Official Source Boundary

Reviewed June 11, 2026.

This training application is not a clinical decision system and its bundled data
has not been professionally validated. The links below are authoritative starting
points for a future qualified content review; adding the links does not validate
the application's current questions, answers, rules, or medication data.

- [PTCB PTCE Content Outline effective January 6, 2026](https://ptcb.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/PTCE-Content-Outline.pdf)
- [DEA Controlled Substances Act overview](https://www.dea.gov/drug-information/csa)
- [CDC immunization schedules for healthcare providers](https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines/hcp/imz-schedules/index.html)
- [FDA Drug Safety Communications](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-safety-and-availability/drug-safety-communications)

Before using content for instruction or assessment, a qualified reviewer should
map each item to a current primary source, record the review date, and rerun the
application's tests after corrections.
